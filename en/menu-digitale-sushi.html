Frequently asked questions

Any doubts?

Can I change the catch of the day?
Yes: request the change from the Support page and we make it for you. Up to 10 changes a month are included and we email you as soon as they are online.
Can I add photos of the dishes?
Yes. Attach a few photos of your dishes to the activation form and we add them to the menu.
How much does the QR code digital menu cost?
The first 30 days from delivery are free. After that the service costs €169 a year, all included (less than €15 a month): personalised menu, QR code, hosting, a web address dedicated to your venue (e.g. dsmenu.it/yourvenue), SSL certificate and up to 10 changes a month. If you prefer, you can pay 2 years for €299 or 3 years for €429 and save up to 15%.
How long does it take to get the menu ready?
About 24 hours from when you send us your menu, even just as a photo attached to the activation form.
