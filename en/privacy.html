Providing data

Providing the data needed for the contract and for support is essential: without it we cannot provide the service or make changes. Other data, such as free-text notes, is optional.

What we do not do

  • We do not use the data for advertising, newsletters or marketing, and we do not transfer or sell it to third parties.
  • To prepare menu drafts we may use an artificial intelligence service (see Providers): it only reads the menu pages, makes no decisions about people, and every draft is checked and published by a person.
  • We do no profiling and make no decisions based solely on automated processing (art. 22 GDPR).
  • We do not process special categories of data (art. 9 GDPR) or data relating to criminal convictions (art. 10 GDPR).

Minors

Our services are aimed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data of children under 14 (art. 2-quinquies Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003).

Free trial activation form

Specific information for those requesting the free trial from the Activation page.

  • After you submit it, we send you an email with a confirmation link, valid for 48 hours. Until you open it, the request is not shown to us as pending; if you do not confirm it, it is deleted after 7 days.
  • Every confirmed request is checked before activation: either by an automated assistant based on artificial intelligence (see Providers), which only checks that the material is the menu of a venue and contains no inappropriate content, or by the owner or an authorised collaborator. If the assistant has doubts, the request stays pending and a person decides. The outcome concerns the activation of the free trial, and you can always ask for it to be reviewed by writing to info@dsfabio.com.
  • If the request is approved, we contact you to prepare the menu and the data is transferred to your customer record; from then on the timeframes of the «Customer management» row apply.
  • We ask you to attach the menu you use today: up to 10 files (JPG, PNG, WebP photos or PDFs up to 10 MB), kept in a private archive. If you wish, you can also upload your logo and a cover image (or ask us in the notes): we use them for the menu and publish them with it. Please do not include data about other people or unnecessary information.
  • The venue’s VAT number is optional: if you provide it, we show it at the bottom of the digital menu.
  • For security we apply limits on submissions per device and overall, and we accept only one open request per email address.
  • The form does not use cookies. So that you don’t lose what you have written if your phone reloads the page, it keeps the completed fields, the chosen template and where the visit came from in the browser tab’s temporary storage (see Cookie policy): they are deleted when you submit the request or close the tab. The data is sent to our database on Supabase (European Union) only when you press the submit button.

Customer support form

Specific information for customers using the menu change request form.

  • The form asks for the customer code shown on the sheet you received: without a valid code the request is not saved. The code is personal: do not share it with others.
  • You can attach up to 5 files (JPG, PNG, WebP photos or PDFs up to 10 MB). Photos are reduced automatically before sending.
  • For security we apply limits on submissions: 5 wrong codes per hour per device, an overall limit on wrong attempts and a maximum number of daily requests per customer. Once the limits are exceeded, the form is temporarily locked.
  • Requests are visible only to the owner and to the sales partner who follows your venue.
  • When the request is completed we send a confirmation email to the address registered for your venue.
  • Please do not include in the notes or attachments data about other people (for example photos in which customers or employees can be recognised) or information not needed for the request.

How we protect the data

Technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (art. 32 GDPR).

  • Always encrypted connections (HTTPS) and SSL certificate on website and menus.
  • Reserved area accessible only with personal credentials; two-step verification is mandatory for the administrator.
  • Access rules enforced directly by the database: each partner sees and edits only their own customers.
  • Attachments in a private archive, viewable only through temporary links.
  • Public sign-up of new accounts disabled: accounts are created only by the owner.
  • Limits against abuse of public forms, email confirmation and manual approval of activation requests, deletion of data at the stated deadlines.
  • Public forms can only submit requests: they cannot read, change or delete any data already in the database.

In the event of a personal data breach, we inform the Italian Data Protection Authority within 72 hours and, if the risk is high, also the people concerned (arts. 33 and 34 GDPR).

Write to info@dsfabio.com. The request is free of charge. We reply within one month, extendable by two months in complex cases (art. 12.3 GDPR); we may ask you to confirm your identity.

The digital menus of the venues that are our customers are static information pages: they do not use cookies, do not ask for data and contain no third-party profiling, advertising or analytics tools. For this reason no consent banner is needed.

No IP addresses, devices or other data identifying the viewer are stored: it is anonymous first-party statistics, treated as technical tools by the Italian Data Protection Authority’s Guidelines of 10 June 2021.

  • The buttons to call, write on WhatsApp, open Google Maps, reviews or the venue’s social media lead to external services only when you tap them: from then on the rules of those services apply.
  • The menu content (dishes, prices, allergens, venue details) is provided by the venue, which is responsible for it.

How to delete them

You can delete local storage and cookies at any time from your browser settings, under Privacy or Site data. The website will keep working: you will just be shown the notice again.

If in future we introduce tools that require consent, we will activate them only after asking for it with a banner compliant with the Italian Data Protection Authority’s Guidelines, with the option to refuse as easily as to accept.
