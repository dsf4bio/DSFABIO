For any request about your data, write to info@dsfabio.com or on WhatsApp at +39 344 030 1412.

We don't use your data for advertising or newsletters and we don't sell it to anyone.

How data reaches us and where it is stored

  • WhatsApp: the “Try it free” buttons open WhatsApp with a pre-written message; the website saves nothing and the data only reaches us if you send the message yourself. WhatsApp is operated by WhatsApp Ireland Ltd. (Meta group).
  • Google: the support forms send the request to a Google Sheet via Google Apps Script (Google Ireland Ltd./Google LLC), acting as service provider.
  • Hosting: the website is hosted by Vercel Inc., which logs technical data (IP address, date and time, requested page, browser) for the operation and security of the website (legitimate interest, Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Some of these providers may also process data outside the EU: in that case the transfer takes place with the safeguards set out in Articles 45 and 46 GDPR (for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long

Requests from people who don't become customers are kept for 12 months and then deleted. Customer data is kept for the duration of the service and for the periods required by law for tax documents.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and to object to processing (Articles 15–22 GDPR) by writing to info@dsfabio.com. We reply within 30 days, as required by the GDPR. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante).

2. Cookies

This website does not use profiling, analytics or advertising cookies and does not use analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

  • Fonts and libraries: fonts, images and libraries are hosted directly on this website: no Google Fonts, CDN or other external services are contacted while you browse. Only the customer support forms send the request to Google, as explained above.
  • Cookie notice: when you close it, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage, so we don't show it to you again. This data also stays only on your device.
  • Sample menus: some templates remember the “list of chosen dishes” on your phone using the browser's local storage. The data stays only on your device and is not sent to us.

You can delete this data at any time from your browser settings.

3. Terms and conditions

Terms designed to be clear, fair and transparent. Written agreements made with an individual customer prevail over this page.

The service

We create and manage QR code digital menus for food and drink venues: custom menu, QR code, icons for the 14 allergens, hosting, a dedicated web address and SSL certificate, up to 4 changes a month to dishes and prices, support on WhatsApp with a reply within a few hours on working days.

Free trial

  • It lasts 30 days from delivery of the menu and requires no payment details.
  • At the end of the trial, if you want to continue, the service costs €190 a year.
  • If you don't continue, you pay nothing: the menu is paused (anyone scanning the QR code sees a page with your contact details) and you get the PDF of your menu for free.
  • We recommend always keeping a printed backup copy of the menu.

Renewal and cancellation

We let you know before the annual renewal. You can cancel anytime, without penalties: the service stays active until the end of the period already paid.

Content and allergens

  • Dishes, prices, photos, logo and allergen information are provided and confirmed by the venue, which remains responsible for their accuracy.
  • The content of your menu (texts, photos, logo) remains your property; you can use it freely.

Limitations

  • We do our best to keep the menu online at all times, but we cannot guarantee the operation of third-party services (for example WhatsApp, hosting or the venue's internet connection).
  • Support covers the digital menu, not the devices or connections of the venue and its guests.

Changes

If we update these terms we change the date at the top; changes never reduce services already paid for.
